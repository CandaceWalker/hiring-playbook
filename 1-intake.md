# 1. Intake Meeting

The intake is where most searches are won or lost. The goal is to leave with a shared definition of "great," a realistic plan, and agreement on how you'll work together.

**Length:** 30–45 minutes · **Who:** Recruiter and hiring manager (plus a lead interviewer, if helpful)

## Before the meeting
- Read the job description and note anything vague or contradictory.
- Look at 3–5 profiles of people who already do this job well (on the team or in the market).
- Bring a rough sense of market supply and compensation for the role.

## Questions to ask

**The role**
- Why is this role open now? What happens if it stays open for three months?
- What will this person own in their first 90 days? First year?
- What level is this, really, based on scope, not title?

**The bar**
- What are the 3 must-haves this person cannot succeed without?
- What's nice to have but trainable?
- Who on your team (or elsewhere) is a great example of this role, and why?
- What would make you say "no" to an otherwise strong candidate?

**The market**
- Which companies or teams have people like this?
- Where have you seen great people come from in the past?
- Are we open to adjacent backgrounds? Which ones?

**The process**
- Who's on the interview panel, and what will each person assess?
- How quickly can you review profiles and give feedback?
- Who makes the final decision?

## Leave with agreement on
- [ ] 3 must-haves and 2–3 nice-to-haves
- [ ] Target companies and backgrounds
- [ ] Interview loop and who covers what
- [ ] Service-level agreements (see below)
- [ ] Compensation range and level
- [ ] A date for the first calibration check-in (usually after 5–8 profiles)

## Service-level agreements (SLAs)
Agree on response times so searches don't stall:

| Step | Owner | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Review submitted profiles | Hiring manager | 2 business days |
| Submit interview feedback | Interviewers | Same day as the interview |
| Hold the debrief | Hiring team | Within 2 business days of the final interview |
| Candidate updates | Recruiter | Within 2 business days of any decision |

## After the meeting
Send a one-page summary: must-haves, nice-to-haves, target profiles, interview loop, SLAs, and next check-in date. Ask the hiring manager to confirm it.
