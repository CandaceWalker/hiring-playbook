# 5. Candidate Communication

Candidates remember how you made them feel, especially when the answer is no. Clear, timely, human communication protects your reputation and keeps great people open to future roles.

## Standards
- **Respond within 2 business days** at every stage.
- **Tell candidates what to expect:** stages, timeline, who they'll meet.
- **Never ghost.** Every candidate who interviews gets a clear answer.
- **Close the loop personally** for anyone who reached the final round.

## Templates
Personalize the [brackets]. Keep it short and genuine.

### Interview confirmation
> Hi [Name], thanks for making time to meet the team. Here's what to expect on [date]:
> - [Time]: [Interviewer, role] will focus on [topic]
> - [Time]: [Interviewer, role] will focus on [topic]
>
> [Logistics: link or address, what to prepare]. If anything comes up or you need an accommodation, just let me know. Looking forward to it!

### Status update (when things are taking longer)
> Hi [Name], I wanted to give you a quick update. The team is still [finishing interviews / reviewing feedback], and I expect to have news by [date]. Thanks for your patience, and feel free to reach out with any questions.

### Decline after a screen
> Hi [Name], thank you for taking the time to talk with me about [role]. After careful thought, we've decided not to move forward. I appreciated learning about [something specific], and I'd be glad to stay in touch for future roles that might be a better fit.

### Decline after final interviews
Call first if you can, then follow up in writing:
> Hi [Name], thank you again for the time you spent with the team. This was a hard decision, and we've decided to move forward with another candidate. The team especially appreciated [something specific]. I'd love to keep in touch, and I'll reach out if a role opens up that fits your background.

### Offer call checklist
- [ ] Share excitement and why the team chose them
- [ ] Walk through the offer: base, bonus, equity, benefits, start date
- [ ] Ask what else they need to decide
- [ ] Agree on a date to follow up
- [ ] Send the written offer the same day
