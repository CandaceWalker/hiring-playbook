# 2. Interview Loop

A structured loop means every candidate for a role is assessed on the same competencies, with the same core questions, by interviewers who know exactly what they're covering. It's fairer, faster, and makes debriefs easier.

## Principles
- **Every must-have is assessed by at least one interviewer,** and ideally two.
- **Every interviewer has a clear focus.** No one should be "just getting a feel for the person."
- **No duplicate coverage by accident.** If two people assess the same thing, it should be on purpose.
- **Same core questions for every candidate** in the same role.
- **Respect the candidate's time.** Keep the total loop as short as you can while still covering the must-haves.

## Example loop: Senior Software Engineer

| Stage | Interviewer | Focus | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recruiter screen | Recruiter | Motivation, background, logistics, compensation | 30 min |
| Hiring manager screen | Engineering Manager | Scope of past work, how they make decisions | 45 min |
| Technical exercise | Senior Engineer | Coding, problem-solving, testing habits | 60 min |
| System design | Staff or Senior Engineer | Designing reliable, scalable systems; trade-offs | 60 min |
| Collaboration | Product Manager or Designer | Working across functions, handling disagreement | 45 min |
| Values and growth | Engineering Manager or peer | Ownership, feedback, mentoring | 45 min |

## Brief your interviewers
Before the first candidate, share with each interviewer:
- Their focus area and the must-haves they own
- Their core questions
- The scorecard (see `3-scorecard.md`)
- When feedback is due (same day)

## Questions to never ask
Don't ask about age, family plans, pregnancy, marital status, religion, national origin, disability, health, or arrest records. Ask about the work, and only about what's needed for the job. If you're unsure whether a question is allowed, check with HR or legal first.
